FISH AND CHIPS WITH A TWIST

⌄

Serving a creative style of Fish and Chips

Simply Hooked is a New York based location supplier serving a fresh twist on fish and chips, inspired by the culinary traditions of Ireland and the Basque Region of France.

We offer food and drink venues in New York a refreshingly new additional revenue opportunity, by providing these locations with the ability to offer their customers a menu of freshly prepared coated fish and chips.
Our product can be added to existing menus or the location can offer our menu to their customers. Using our service, venues can place orders straight to our kitchens and the product is delivered directly to their location.

All the dishes are made using only sensibly sourced produce and sustainably caught fish. Then the food is freshly prepared to order.
Customers at the locations can create their own food experience by first selecting a locally sourced, sustainably caught fish and then choosing their preferred coating, which ranges from the classic Tempura finish to a spicy Wasabi crust.

Our Commitment

Simply Hooked is committed to serving only sustainable seafood. In addition we are always looking at ways of reducing our plastic footprint, one of these ways is by serving in compostable take-away containers. Ocean bound plastic pollution is a growing environmental threat with over 10 million tons of new plastic entering our ocean each year. Simply Hooked ensures that any packaging for its product is collected and recycled.
