Serving a creative style of Fish and Chips

Simply Hooked is a New York based location supplier serving a fresh twist on fish and chips, inspired by the culinary traditions of Ireland and the Basque Region of France.

We offer food and drink venues in New York a refreshingly new additional revenue opportunity, providing locations with the ability to offer their customers freshly prepared coated fish and chips. Our product can be added to existing menus or the location can offer our menu directly. Venues place orders straight to our kitchens and the product is delivered to their location.

All dishes are made using sensibly sourced produce and sustainably caught fish, freshly prepared to order. Customers create their own food experience by selecting a locally sourced fish and choosing a preferred coating, from classic tempura to a Sri Lankan spiced crust.

Our Commitment

Simply Hooked is committed to serving only sustainable seafood. We are always looking at ways of reducing our plastic footprint, including serving in compostable take-away containers. Ocean-bound plastic pollution is a growing environmental threat, with over 10 million tons of new plastic entering our ocean each year. Simply Hooked ensures that product packaging is collected and recycled.
