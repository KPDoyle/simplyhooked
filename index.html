A fresh twist, delivered to your venue

A proven favourite, evolved

Simply Hooked is a New York based location supplier serving a fresh twist on fish and chips, inspired by the culinary traditions of Ireland and the Basque Region of France.

02

A simple additional revenue stream

Food and drink venues can add our dishes to an existing menu or offer the Simply Hooked menu directly. Orders go straight to our kitchens and are delivered to the location.

03

Made to order

Customers select a locally sourced, sustainably caught fish and choose a coating, from classic tempura to a Sri Lankan spiced or dill and lime panko crust.

SH

Our commitment

Good fish should leave a lighter footprint

Simply Hooked is committed to serving only sustainable seafood. We continually look for ways to reduce our plastic footprint, including compostable takeaway containers and the collection and recycling of our packaging.

Ocean-bound plastic pollution is a growing environmental threat. Every choice—from the species we serve to the way each dish is packed—is part of our responsibility to the ocean.
